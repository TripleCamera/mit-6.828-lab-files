At the end of the term, I added testtime, testoutput, and testinput.
They're all integrated into the grade script already, but need to be
mentioned in the lab writeup.  testinput has the odd caveat that
output needs to be working for at least one packet (since it needs to
transmit an ARP packet).

Make it clear that "make nc-7" does not itself run QEMU.  A surprising
number of students were confused by the "connection refused" messages
produced by "make nc-7" when they hadn't started QEMU.

Fix lwip thread scheduling.  The lwip environment blocks on the IPC
receive even when there is working waiting in the other cooperative
threads.  That's why the echo is so slow (it ultimately gets pushed
through by the timer IPC) and why Adam's got behind without the timer
IPC.

The E100 spec is frustratingly impenetrable, and seems to be doubly so
for students who have never seen a spec before.  It's written from the
perspective of building the card and not at all from the perspective
of controlling the card.  I found myself constantly flipping between
three or so places in the reference while writing the driver.  Some
sort of cross-reference that highlights the important parts and puts
them in a sensible order for driver-writing may prove extremely
helpful.
