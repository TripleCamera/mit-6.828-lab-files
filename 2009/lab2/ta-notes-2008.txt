Subject: common points of confusion for lab 2

After helping some students with lab 2 I think I've found some things that caused 
problems for a lot of them. 

1. Many students do not remember lab 1. They do not remember that we mapped 
   0x00000000 to 0xf0000000 using segmentation, so in lab 2 they can use KADDR 
   and PADDR to convert between physical and virtual addresses. They think since 
   we have not started using paging yet, physical address must be equal to 
   virtual addresses. Perhaps we need to stress more how important understanding 
   lab 1 is for lab 2. 

2. Many students think that the kernel can use physical addresses. I can't tell 
   if this is because in lab 2 paging has not been turned on yet, or if this is 
   a more general misconception.

3. Many students cannot tell which limitations are due to design decisions made 
   specifically for JOS, or just inherent limitations. For example, many students 
   are confused about the way the virtual address space is laid out and why JOS 
   can only address 256 MB of memory. This was explained in lab 1, but many 
   students forgot and are now very confused.
